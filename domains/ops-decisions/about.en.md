# D-ND Lab — MetaMasterLab

## About

MetaMasterLab is the system Lab that governs the evolution of D-ND Labs. It
does not study an external domain such as markets, physics or biosignals: it
observes Labs as living tools and checks whether they are producing real
difference or only noise, sterile symmetry, tautological all-pass states and
intent drift.

Its central question is:

> are the initial intent, seed, reports, falsifier and resultant still
> describing the same movement, or is the Lab growing without preserving its
> essence?

The legacy `ops-decisions` core remains valid and compatible: incident
regeneration and decision archaeology become two internal sensors of the
MetaMasterLab. Incidents show where a Lab no longer sees a boundary; historical
decisions show which rules the operator already applies before the system has
formalized them.

The Lab does not impose changes and does not directly mutate target Labs. It
proposes verifiable evolution decisions: which tension to close, which guard
should become an assertion, which UI surface is missing, which claim should be
degraded to a constraint, and which Lab should be used as a D-ND tool for an
intent that is not yet explicitly formulated.

Planned future outputs, not mandatory yet:

- `evolution_decision.json`: proposed next move for a target Lab.
- `lab_steward_report.md`: compact read of the Lab's evolutionary state.
- `intent_drift_check.json`: distance between intent, seed, reports and
  resultant.
- `exponentiality_score.json`: useful difference vs noise accumulation.
- `tool_selection_proposal.json`: which Lab to use as a D-ND tool in an
  applied context.

Meta-lab generates Labs and templates. MetaMasterLab governs live Labs so that
evolutionary exponentiality prevails over noise and symmetry without losing the
essence of the original movement.
